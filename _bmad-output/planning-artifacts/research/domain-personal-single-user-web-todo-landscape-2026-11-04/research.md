# Domain research: personal single-user web todo landscape

**Decision this research serves:** Which existing web products already cover most of one person’s todo job (no TAM, no collaboration, no native app).

Light run. Headless. Subagents none (caller forbade extra agents). Depth 1. Official help pages, accessed 2026-11-04.

## Products

**Todoist** (web). Weekday Today view; four priorities, with priority 1 near the top of Today under timed tasks; task descriptions; reminders; duplicate; Google or Outlook events shown with tasks in Today and Upcoming. Calendar layouts listed are day, week, and month.

**TickTick** (web). High/medium/low priority, pin, duration, reminders, duplicate, day/week/month/year views, and subscribed Google Calendar events in Today.

**Microsoft To Do** (web). My Day for a morning selection, importance star, notes, reminders, steps, and an All list.

**Remember The Milk** (web). Priority, time estimates, notes, reminders, and smart lists for incomplete, high-priority, or short tasks. Tags can name people.

**Google Tasks** inside Google Calendar (web). Descriptions, subtasks, date/time notifications, a planned duration, and day/week/month/year views that also show other calendars.

## Sourced gaps

Todoist Today and Upcoming omit tasks with no date. The official layout list has no year view. Duplicate copies omit comments and custom reminders.

TickTick records partial completion as a percentage, including an automatic percent from check items. Year view shows only dated tasks. Persistent alarm reminders are documented for the mobile app; the web help path is email notification.

Google Tasks’ Pending list is uncompleted tasks from the past 365 days. The documented task settings do not include a priority field.

Remember The Milk’s documented calendar is an iCalendar feed of due-dated tasks into another calendar app.

Microsoft’s help documents moving a task to another list, not copying it.

## Unknown

A confirm-before-copy step in any of these products. Whether Microsoft To Do shows other people’s fixed times, or day/week/month/year views. Whether Remember The Milk can copy items between lists.
