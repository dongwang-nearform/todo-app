# Addendum

Withdrawn decisions and the calendar non-goal, kept out of the PRD body.

## Calendar non-goal

Calendar add was specified, then removed from v1. It is not deferred work. Do not design a Google Calendar or Apple Calendar integration, and do not design an in-product calendar. The alarm remains a product capability. It is shown on the to-do and is never fired, so do not design lead time or delivery.

## Withdrawn decisions

- **Saturday English clock:** An earlier narration put Saturday English at 8:00 AM. That clock is withdrawn. Drop-off is 9:00 AM and pickup is 10:00 AM.
- **Native phone support:** It was in an earlier form-factor decision and was removed. v1 is one responsive website.
